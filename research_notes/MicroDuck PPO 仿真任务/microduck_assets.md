# MicroDuck simulation asset survey

## What is already physically available in DuckKing and the upstream model

### Takeaway
DuckKing already exposes a CPU MuJoCo backend around the pinned Pollen MicroDuck assets. The highest-return PPO extensions should reuse the existing 14-servo observation/action contract and scene builders rather than introduce a new robot model. Upstream `microduck_rl` confirms that the official task family already covers velocity locomotion, fall recovery, stand-up, sit/stand, ground pick, ball kick, roulade, roller skating, roller crouch, roller slope and spin.

### Cited Findings
- Upstream describes MicroDuck as an approximately 800 g, 25 cm biped with 14 Dynamixel XL330 servos; policies are trained at 50 Hz with PPO, exported to ONNX, and deployed by the MicroDuck runtime. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- Upstream task registry lists `Mjlab-Velocity-{Flat,Rough}-MicroDuck` for velocity-command walking and head pose, `Mjlab-VelStand-{Flat,Rough}-MicroDuck` for walking plus fall recovery, `Mjlab-StandUp-{Flat,Rough}-MicroDuck`, `Mjlab-SitStand-{Flat,Rough}-MicroDuck`, `Mjlab-GroundPick-{Flat,Rough}-MicroDuck`, `Mjlab-BallKick-Flat-MicroDuck`, `Mjlab-Roulade-Flat-MicroDuck`, four roller tasks, and `Mjlab-Spin-Flat-MicroDuck`. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- Upstream exports five robot-model families relevant to PPO: `robot_walk.xml`, `robot_groundcontact.xml`, `robot_groundcontact_rollers.xml`, `robot_allcollisions.xml`, and generated backlash variants. The ground-contact model is used for stand/recovery/tricks, rollers adds passive wheels, and all-collisions is available for true contact experiments. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- The upstream actuator path models the Dynamixel XL330 with BAM voltage control, load-dependent friction, back-EMF, command delay, battery-voltage/sag randomization and friction randomization. This is a substantially stronger sim-to-real baseline than ideal position PD. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- Upstream's 61-dimensional actor contract is shared across policies: 48 proprioceptive features plus 13 command dimensions `[twist(3), head_pose(4), body_pose(6)]`; unused command slots are zero padded to keep ONNX hot-swapping possible. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- DuckKing's `Driver` uses the same 14 named joints and `HOME` pose, reads IMU angular velocity and projected gravity, keeps the previous action, accepts a 13-dimensional command vector, and applies residual targets through MuJoCo position actuators. — [DuckKing simulation.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/simulation.py)
- DuckKing's CPU backend loads official ONNX policies for walking, kicking, sit/stand, roulade, ground pick, roller gait and roller crouch; it compiles ball, duo, carry, skills, rollers, table, seated and stage models. — [DuckKing simulation.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/simulation.py)
- The ball scene contains a free ball and verifies selected-foot contact and measured ball displacement after the kick; the backend explicitly adds a ball/floor rolling-resistance contact pair. — [DuckKing simulation.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/simulation.py)
- The roller model uses passive wheel joints and a separate roller crouch policy. DuckKing verifies wheel rotation and displacement for skating and verifies center-of-mass height change for roller crouch. — [DuckKing official_skills.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/official_skills.py), [DuckKing skills_model.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/skills_model.py)
- DuckKing's carry scene adds an explicitly experimental articulated beak, upper/lower contact pads, a 4 g free object, source stand and destination stand. Its verifier measures bilateral pad force, lift clearance, grip loss/slip, carry distance, destination contact and stable release; the code labels the mouth mechanism `experimental` rather than official calibration. — [DuckKing grasp_model.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/grasp_model.py), [DuckKing grasp.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/grasp.py)
- DuckKing's table/seated scenes instantiate four attached MicroDucks and use real floor/table contact, measured posture, bounded drift and per-singer stand/sit confirmations. The stage scene adds four ducks, random non-overlapping spawns, bounded A* navigation, actual inter-duck contact, poke forces, rolling and recovery. — [DuckKing table_choir.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/table_choir.py), [DuckKing seated_choir.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/seated_choir.py), [DuckKing stage.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/stage.py)

### Inferences
- A PPO environment should preserve the existing 14-joint order and command slots. The current DuckKing_PPO environment has a 59-dimensional custom observation; adding a compatibility wrapper that emits the upstream 61-dimensional contract would make learned policies easier to compare with and potentially hot-swap against official policies.
- The fastest path to useful experiments is to wrap already-compiled groundcontact/rollers/carry scenes with task-specific rewards and reset distributions. New assets are only needed for richer object interaction, deformable objects, camera observations or new terrain.
- BAM/backlash fidelity should be treated as a second-stage sim-to-real upgrade. The current DuckKing_PPO residual-PD setup is suitable for CPU prototyping, while the upstream stack is the reference for hardware-relevant training.

### Gaps
- The current public upstream README documents task names and model families but does not provide a single CPU Stable-Baselines3 entry point; the upstream training stack uses mjlab/MuJoCo Warp and PPO. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- The exact licensed policy weights and model distribution terms should be checked before redistributing learned ONNX files; the upstream repository separates code and model licenses. — [microduck_rl LICENSE-MODELS](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/LICENSE-MODELS.md)

## Which PPO tasks are practical next for this project

### Takeaway
There are three tiers. Tier 1 can be implemented by changing resets, rewards and wrappers around assets already present: rough/slope locomotion, fall recovery, sit-to-stand, roller slope/spin and kick accuracy. Tier 2 needs modest task geometry or state-machine additions: beak pickup/carry, ball dribbling, target navigation, multi-duck collision avoidance and coordinated transport. Tier 3 is research-heavy: visual control, system-identification/domain-adaptation, dynamic manipulation and multi-agent learned choreography.

### Cited Findings
- Upstream already has rough-terrain variants for velocity, velstand, standup, sitstand and ground pick, so rough-terrain locomotion and recovery are established task families rather than speculative ideas. — [microduck_rl README](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/README.md)
- Upstream includes a curriculum-generated flat ramp for roller slope and tests terrain difficulty progression, initial ramp spawn, runout geometry and void/fall termination. — [roller slope tests](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/tests/test_roller_slope_cfg.py), [slope terrain tests](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/tests/test_slope_terrain.py)
- Upstream's spin task tracks angular rate, stay-in-place, wheel differential, grounded support and leg anti-symmetry, and disables left/right symmetry augmentation because mirroring changes spin direction. — [spin task tests](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/tests/test_spin_cfg.py)
- Upstream's roller stand-up starts from prone/back/standing mixtures, uses a six-second episode, a measured roller standing height, a reversed rolling-friction curriculum and recovery-specific rewards. — [roller stand-up policy summary](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/docs/roller_standup_policy_summary.md), [roller stand-up tests](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/tests/test_roller_standup_cfg.py)
- Upstream ground pick defines a task-space target for mouth-to-ground proximity, mouth orientation, feet support, no unintended head impact and return-to-stand; it uses a phase command and a sampled payload event. — [ground-pick config tests](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/tests/test_ground_pick_cfg.py)
- DuckKing's existing carry verifier gives direct state variables for a learned pickup/carry reward: bilateral contact duration, object clearance, grip-relative slip, robot/object travel and stable release. — [DuckKing grasp.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/grasp.py)
- DuckKing's stage code already computes obstacle-aware routes around other duck positions, bounds the floor, tracks contacts and verifies roll/recovery. — [DuckKing stage.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/stage.py)

### Inferences
- **Recommended first implementation: disturbance recovery benchmark.** Add a task variant to the current balance env with random pushes/torques, random initial tilt and rough/slope patches. It requires no new actuator model, produces a clear metric (recovery success/time/peak tilt), and directly extends the current turntable work.
- **Recommended second: roller slope and spin.** Reuse `robot_groundcontact_rollers.xml`, add a ramp or prescribed angular-rate command, and reward downhill heading/desired spin while penalizing lateral drift. The upstream tests provide concrete geometry and reward guardrails.
- **Recommended third: ball skill suite.** Start with kick target accuracy (direction, distance, selected-foot contact), then progress to ball interception/dribble. The existing ball body/contact verifier means the first task needs mostly randomized targets and reward shaping, not new robot geometry.
- **Recommended fourth: stand-up/pose transition.** Use prone/back/sitting resets and progress/recovery rewards, but follow upstream’s measured height and contact gates. Do not reward low-body height or generic contact without an end-state gate because a stable flop can farm reward.
- **Recommended fifth: beak pickup and carry.** The current experimental mouth pads and free object are enough for a PPO prototype. Shape rewards around bilateral contact and incremental lift/carry progress, cap progress rewards, and terminate grip loss. A curriculum over object mass/friction/target distance is straightforward.
- **Recommended sixth: navigation and multi-duck coordination.** Learn local velocity commands or target-reaching around other ducks, then add collision avoidance and cooperative formation. Existing four-duck stage geometry, contacts and A* route can provide demonstrations/teacher trajectories and evaluation metrics.
- **Higher-risk extensions:** visual policy from camera images, learned sim-to-real system identification, deformable/soft objects, and hierarchical policy switching. These need sensor/rendering changes, more data and stronger validation than the current CPU PPO setup.

### Gaps
- No local benchmark yet compares PPO against the official ONNX policies under identical random seeds, actuator models and observation normalization. Build that evaluator before claiming a learned task is better.
- There is no local measurement of compute budget for GPU-scale mjlab training versus CPU Stable-Baselines3. The current repository is suitable for smoke tests and small studies, not thousands of parallel MuJoCo Warp environments.
- Multi-agent PPO algorithm choice (centralized critic, parameter sharing, independent PPO or hierarchical planner) remains open; the current DuckKing orchestration is scripted rather than learned.

## What implementation order gives the best joint project

### Takeaway
A staged roadmap can produce publishable results while steadily increasing realism: (1) recovery benchmark, (2) slope/roller locomotion, (3) kick/dribble, (4) pickup/carry, (5) multi-duck navigation, (6) sim-to-real and vision. Every stage should include deterministic evaluation, randomized holdout conditions, video plus telemetry, and a fixed official-policy baseline.

### Cited Findings
- Upstream explicitly recommends a 64-environment, five-iteration smoke test before long training, and its task workflow requires CPU config tests, NaN guards, observation parity, ONNX export and domain-randomization checks. — [microduck_rl AGENTS.md](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/AGENTS.md)
- Upstream states that reward terms must be audited for sign, that under-specified dynamic skills are reward-hacked, that progress rewards should be rate-limited, and that hard state-based gates are required for tricks and end states. — [microduck_rl AGENTS.md](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/AGENTS.md)
- Upstream reports that stand-up rewards must use measured robot-specific heights, that actuator backlash must be modeled through encoder observations, and that policies should keep a shared observation layout for deployment. — [microduck_rl AGENTS.md](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/blob/develop/AGENTS.md)
- DuckKing's physical backend explicitly differentiates real MuJoCo contact verification from preview animation and reports contact, posture, displacement and recovery outcomes. — [DuckKing simulation.py](https://github.com/zhoutingunl/DuckKing/blob/dba47d4/src/duckking/simulation.py)

### Inferences
- For the current repository, each new task should ship with: an environment config, a reward/termination unit test, a short smoke training command, a deterministic holdout evaluation script, a visualization artifact and a comparison report against the official ONNX controller.
- The next branch should add a generic disturbance/terrain/object interface to `MicroDuckBalanceEnv` instead of creating separate monolithic environments. Parameters such as terrain, platform motion, push schedule and payload should be resettable and reportable through `info`.
- The most valuable near-term study is not “can PPO do another trick?” but “how much robustness is gained by curriculum + domain randomization + actuator fidelity?” Run ablations on flat/static, tilted/static, nonuniform rotation, rough terrain, backlash and BAM actuator models.
- A practical milestone is a suite of five tasks with common 61-D observations and 14-D residual actions: balance, recovery, slope/roller, kick, and pickup. This supports one evaluator, one visualization pipeline and direct policy interchange tests.

### Gaps
- Hardware transfer has not been performed in DuckKing_PPO; all proposals remain simulation-only until a separately safety-limited real-robot test is authorized.
- The current custom environment uses a 59-D observation and ideal MuJoCo position actuators; compatibility with upstream 61-D ONNX policies and BAM actuators is a planned enhancement, not a completed fact.
- The survey cannot determine which tasks are most valuable to the user’s product/demo goals; that prioritization should be chosen from the roadmap after reviewing expected training time and desired visual demonstrations.
